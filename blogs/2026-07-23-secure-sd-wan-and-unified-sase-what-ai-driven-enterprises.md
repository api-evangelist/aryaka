Every enterprise network built in the last two decades was designed around the same assumption: traffic is predictable, applications live in a data center, and humans generate the load. AI just broke that assumption. Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, agentic workflows, and retrieval-augmented generation don’t behave like the traffic MPLS or first-generation SD-WAN were built for.
