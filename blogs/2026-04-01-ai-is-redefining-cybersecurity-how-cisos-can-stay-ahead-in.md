Artificial intelligence is no longer an emerging trend—it is actively reshaping cybersecurity. For enterprise CISOs, AI introduces a dual challenge: enabling innovation while managing a rapidly expanding attack surface. Employees are adopting AI tools at scale, while adversaries are leveraging the same technology to accelerate and refine attacks.
